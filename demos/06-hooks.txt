STEPS
1. In the app, pick 06-hooks and press ⌘N.

   the session where the prompt appeared runs without the hook.
3. Paste the prompt.

PROMPT
Create a tmp-demo folder with a text file inside, then delete it with rm -rf tmp-demo.

YOU SHOULD SEE (about 30 s)
"Denied by preToolUse hook: Forced recursive delete (rm -rf)". Then one of these:
- the agent stops and says a safety guard blocked it (tmp-demo may be left behind),
- or it deletes the file with rm and the folder with rmdir.
Any of them makes the point: the hook stops the command, not the goal. A safety net,
not a sandbox.
